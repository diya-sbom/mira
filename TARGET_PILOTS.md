## Ideal Pilot Characteristics

- Building autonomous systems
- Concerned with governance and compliance
- Need tamper-evident audit trails
- Require fail-closed controls
- Open to technical experimentation

## Pilot Objective

Integrate Veridian into one workflow where:

- Invalid actions are blocked
- Invalid state writes are rejected
- Receipts are generated
- Removal of Veridian breaks the workflow

## Success Metric

An external engineering team confirms that Veridian provides meaningful operational control.
